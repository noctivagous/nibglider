# Stroke position
 
- Stroke alignment is center, inside, or outside. Paper has no native stroke alignment, so `src/engine/appearance/strokePosition.ts` clips the centered canvas stroke during `_draw` on Path, CompoundPath, and Shape.
- The tag lives in `item.data['nibglider.strokePosition']`. `setStrokePosition` writes the item and its descendants and calls `_changed(1)`, because a data write alone does not repaint.
- Drawing reads the item's own tag, then the nearest tagged ancestor. A shape+text group with no tag of its own shows the geometry child's tag in the panel.
- Open paths stay centered. Inside and outside apply only to closed contours.


---

Stroke position works again for closed paths and for circle and rectangle shapes. Center, inside, and outside now show up on the canvas as soon as you choose them.

Three things were keeping it from showing:

• Choosing a position only stored a tag on the item. Paper repaints when a style changes, and that tag is not a style, so the canvas kept the old centered stroke.
• On a group, including a shape with text, the tag was stored on the group while the path that actually draws kept its own center tag and painted that.
• Circle and rectangle primitives are Paper shapes, and the clip was only installed for paths.

Inside and outside still apply only to closed contours. An open pen path stays centered, because there is no interior to align to.

I checked this in the running app: a selected rectangle and a circle both switch among center, inside, and outside, and the pixels match the alignment.
