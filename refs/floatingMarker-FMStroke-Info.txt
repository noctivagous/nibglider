We found in the project Floating Marker that deposited points are about corners and then the segment that follows will relate to the corner.  So what we had was hardCorner, what you would make a polygon out of because the corner was hard and the segment was straight.  Then we had roundCorner, which was not an arc-based corner but just smooth and extending from it was a straight segment.  Then we had hardCorner-bowedLine and that was where we bowed the segment that followed the hard corner.  rounder-Corner-bowedLine bowed the segment that followed the roundCorner.  The bow would be on either side of the straight line so we had two keys for each: hardCorner-bowedLine-A hardCorner-bowedLine-B and  rounder-Corner-bowedLine-A and  rounder-Corner-bowedLine-B.  Separate from this was an arc point and it would draw an arc for the segment.  for the bowed lines, it relies on the curve produced by the spline and artificial points that we made that made the bowed line.  Then we had a separate bSpline key that deposited single.  The key is that we took the b-spline and made use of it into a composite spline structure we called FMStroke, which was built on top of Apple's PKStroke but we used its resources for vector-drawing.  We don't just use splines raw as they are given but instead direct them within a composite structure like described, because only this way can we have live drawing with splines that is uninterrupted.   We know that the clothoid has been utilized for fluid vector drawing, though not in key-click situations, so we are interested in seeing what it can offer as well.
